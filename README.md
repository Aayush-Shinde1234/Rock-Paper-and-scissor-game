# Rock, Paper, Scissors Game

A fun Python command-line game where you play Rock, Paper, Scissors against the computer.

## Features

* Play against a computer opponent
* Random computer choices
* Score tracking for both player and computer
* Multiple rounds supported
* Simple and interactive gameplay

## Requirements

* Python 3.x

## How to Play

1. Enter one of the following:

   * Rock
   * Paper
   * Scissors
2. The computer will randomly choose its move.
3. The winner of the round is announced.
4. Scores are updated after each round.
5. Choose whether to play again.

## Example

Enter your choice here : Rock

Computer wins

Your choice : Rock
Computer choice : Paper
Score → You: 0 | Computer: 1

Do you want to play again? (yes/no): yes

## Rules

* Rock beats Scissors
* Scissors beats Paper
* Paper beats Rock
* Same choices result in a draw
